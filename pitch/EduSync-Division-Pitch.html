The Challenge

What Schools Are Facing Today

Philippine public schools face overwhelming administrative burdens that consume hundreds of hours annually — time that should be spent on teaching and learning.

📝

Manual Form Generation

Teachers spend 2-3 weeks preparing SF1, SF2, SF9, Form 137, Form 138 — all manually on paper or Excel.

🔄

Duplicate Data Entry

The same student data is entered multiple times across different forms and systems — EBEIS, LIS, manual records.

📊

No Real-Time Insights

Division officers wait weeks or months for aggregated reports. At-risk students go unidentified until it's too late.

🏫

Fragmented Systems

Schools use different tools — Google Sheets, Facebook groups, paper — with no unified system across the division.

📶

Connectivity Issues

Rural schools have unreliable internet. Cloud-only systems fail, causing data loss and frustration.

👨‍👩‍👧

Disconnected Parents

Parents have no visibility into their child's grades, attendance, or school activities until report card day.
