## PROMPT (Copy & Paste into AI Video Tool)

---

Create a professional, cinematic pitch video for **EduSync** — a cloud-based School Information System built specifically for Philippine K-12 schools and DepEd Division Offices.

### TONE & STYLE
- Corporate-professional with a warm, aspirational tone — like a premium SaaS product launch
- Clean motion graphics with smooth transitions between sections
- Color palette: deep navy (#0B1D3A), blue (#1E6FD9), gold accents (#D4A843), and white
- Modern sans-serif typography (Inter, Poppins, or Segoe UI)
- Subtle background music: uplifting, corporate-tech, inspiring (think Salesforce or Google Workspace ads)
- Pace: confident and deliberate — let each point breathe

### VISUAL DIRECTION NOTES

- **UI Showcases**: Use clean dashboard mockups showing student lists, gradebook grids, Form 137 outputs, analytics charts, parent portal views. If using screen recordings, show real-time data loading with smooth scrolls.
- **Human Element**: Include brief clips or AI-generated visuals of Filipino teachers in classrooms, school administrators at desks, parents using phones, students in uniforms — to ground the tech in real-world context.
- **Transitions**: Use smooth slide/fade transitions. No harsh cuts. Each section should flow naturally into the next.
- **Logo Placement**: EduSync logo (shield with graduation cap, navy/blue/gold theme) should appear subtly in the corner throughout and prominently at open and close.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (landscape) for presentations and YouTube; also generate a 9:16 vertical cut for social media/TikTok/Reels.
- **Captions**: Include English subtitles throughout. Optionally add Filipino subtitle track.
- **End Card**: Hold for 5 seconds with logo, tagline, website, and contact email.
