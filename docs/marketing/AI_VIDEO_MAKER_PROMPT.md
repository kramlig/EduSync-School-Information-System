## 💡 PRO TIPS FOR AI VIDEO MAKERS

### For Best Results:
1. **Upload high-quality source footage** (1080p minimum)
2. **Use simple, clear prompts** for each section
3. **Generate in sections** (10 clips) then stitch together
4. **Test different voices** for voiceover (pick best)
5. **Iterate on timing** (some sections may need +/- 2 seconds)
6. **A/B test hooks** (generate 3 different openings, test with audience)

### Common Mistakes to Avoid:
- ❌ Overloading with too much information
- ❌ Using AI-generated faces that look uncanny
- ❌ Generic animations that don't match brand
- ❌ Ignoring mobile viewing experience
- ❌ Weak ending (no clear next step)
