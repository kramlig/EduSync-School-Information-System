## 1. Getting Started

### What is EduSync?

EduSync is a **web-based School Information System** designed for Philippine schools. It follows DepEd standards and helps you manage students, grades, attendance, official school forms, and more — all in one place.

### What You Need

- A computer, tablet, or smartphone with a web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari recommended)
- An internet connection
- Your login credentials (email and password) provided by your school administrator

### Who Uses EduSync?

| Role | What They Can Do |
|------|-----------------|
| **Admin** | Full control — manage students, teachers, grades, reports, settings, finances |
| **Registrar** | Manage student records, enrollment, forms, and financial records |
| **Principal** | View reports, school-wide data, announcements, and oversight tools |
| **Teacher** | Enter grades, record attendance, manage assignments and lesson plans |
| **Student** | View own grades, attendance, assignments, and class schedule |
| **Parent** | Monitor children's grades, attendance, and billing |

## 2. Logging In

### Step-by-Step

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to your school's EduSync website (e.g., `https://edusync.ph`)
3. You will see the **Login Screen**
4. Enter your **email address** and **password**
5. Click **"Log In"**

### Forgot Your Password?

- Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login screen
- Enter your email address
- Check your email inbox for a password reset link
- Follow the instructions in the email to set a new password

### First Time Logging In?

- Your school administrator will give you your email and temporary password
- After logging in for the first time, it is recommended to change your password in your profile settings

### Having Trouble?

- Make sure you are typing your email correctly (check for extra spaces)
- Passwords are case-sensitive — check if Caps Lock is on
- Clear your browser cache if the page seems stuck (press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`)
- Contact your school administrator if you still cannot log in

## 3. Understanding Your Dashboard

After logging in, you will land on your **Dashboard**. This is your home screen.

### Admin Dashboard

Your dashboard shows a quick summary of:
- **Total Students** enrolled in the school
- **Average Attendance** rate for the day
- **Promotion Rate** for the school year
- **Quick links** to common tasks

### Teacher Dashboard

As a teacher, your dashboard shows:
- Your **assigned sections** and subjects
- **Today's schedule** at a glance
- **Pending tasks** (grades to submit, attendance to record)
- **Recent announcements**

### Navigating the System

On the **left side** of the screen, you will see the **Sidebar Menu**. This is your main navigation:

- Click on any menu item to go to that section
- The sidebar groups features into categories:
  - **Main** — Dashboard
  - **School Management** — Students, Teachers, Parents, Sections
  - **Academics** — Grades, Assignments, Lesson Plans, Attendance
  - **Reports & Forms** — DepEd forms, Form 137, Form 138, School Forms
  - **Financial** — Fees, Payments, Receipts (Admin/Registrar only)
  - **System** — Settings, User Management

> **Tip:** If you're looking for a specific page, you can use the sidebar to navigate quickly. Each section is clearly labeled.

## 8. Learning Areas (Subjects)

### What Are Learning Areas?

Learning Areas are the subjects taught in your school, following the DepEd K-12 curriculum. Examples:
- Filipino, English, Mathematics, Science
- Araling Panlipunan, ESP, MAPEH
- EPP/TLE (Grades 4-6), Mother Tongue (Grades 1-3)

### Viewing Learning Areas

1. Click **"Learning Areas"** in the sidebar
2. You will see all subjects configured for your school
3. Each subject shows its code, name, grade levels, and category

### Loading Default DepEd Subjects

If your school is new and has no subjects set up:

1. Go to **Learning Areas**
2. If the list is empty, you will see a **"Load Default DepEd Subjects"** button
3. Click it — the system will automatically add all standard DepEd subjects for your school type (Elementary or Secondary)

### Adding a Custom Subject

1. Click **"Add Learning Area"**
2. Enter:
   - Subject code (e.g., "COMP" for Computer)
   - Subject name
   - Grade levels it applies to
   - Category (Core, TLE, etc.)

1. Click the **edit** icon next to the subject
2. Modify the details and click **"Save"**
3. To remove, click the **delete** icon (this will mark it as inactive, not permanently delete it)

> **Note:** Learning areas are used throughout the system — in gradebooks, report cards, and DepEd forms. Make sure they are accurate before entering grades.

## 9. Grade Entry & Assessment

**Who can access:** Teacher, Admin, Registrar, Principal (view)

### Overview

The Grades module is the heart of academic record-keeping. There are multiple ways to enter and view grades:

| View | Purpose | Best For |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Gradebook** | Enter quarterly grades per subject | Teachers entering final quarter grades |
| **Electronic Class Record (ECR)** | Detailed component-based grading | Teachers who need to track WW, PT, QA components |
| **Core Values** | Character and conduct assessment | Advisers grading behavior, attendance, participation |
| **Analytics** | Grade trends and insights | Admins and principals reviewing school performance |

### Using the Gradebook

1. Click **"Grade Entry"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Academic Gradebook"**
3. Select your **section** and **subject** from the dropdowns
4. You will see a table with all students in that section
5. For each student, enter their grade for each quarter (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)
6. The system automatically calculates the **Final Grade** (average of all quarters)
7. Click **"Save"** to save your entries

### Understanding Grade Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Q1** | First Quarter grade (1-100) |
| **Q2** | Second Quarter grade (1-100) |
| **Q3** | Third Quarter grade (1-100) |
| **Q4** | Fourth Quarter grade (1-100) |
| **Final** | Average of all quarters (auto-calculated) |
| **Remarks** | Passed / Failed (auto-determined based on 75 passing grade) |

### Tips for Grade Entry

- Grades must be whole numbers between **1 and 100**
- The passing grade is **75** (per DepEd Order)
- You don't need to enter all quarters at once — save as you go
- The system will highlight any failing grades in **red** for easy identification

## 10. Electronic Class Record (ECR)

**Who can access:** Teacher

### What is the ECR?

The Electronic Class Record is a detailed grading tool that breaks down grades into DepEd-standard components:

| Component | Abbreviation | Weight |
|-----------|-------------|--------|
| **Written Works** | WW | Varies by subject |
| **Performance Tasks** | PT | Varies by subject |
| **Quarterly Assessment** | QA | 20% (standard) |

### Using the ECR

1. Click **"Grade Entry"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Class Record Selector"**
3. Select your **section** and **learning area** (subject)
4. You will be taken to the Class Record view

### Entering Grades in the ECR

1. For each student, you will see columns for:
   - Individual Written Works (WW1, WW2, WW3, etc.)
   - Individual Performance Tasks (PT1, PT2, PT3, etc.)
   - Quarterly Assessment score
2. Enter the raw scores for each activity
3. The system automatically computes:
   - **Percentage Score** per component
   - **Weighted Score** per component
   - **Initial Grade** (sum of weighted scores)
   - **Quarterly Grade** (transmuted grade based on DepEd transmutation table)

### Adding New Activities

1. At the top of each component section (WW or PT), click **"+ Add"**
2. Enter the activity name and the **Highest Possible Score (HPS)**
3. Click **"Save"**

> **Tip:** The ECR follows DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015 grading guidelines. Weights are pre-configured per subject but can be adjusted by the admin.

## 11. Core Values Assessment

**Who can access:** Teacher (Adviser), Admin

### What Are Core Values?

DepEd requires schools to assess students on behavioral traits beyond academics:

- **Maka-Diyos** (Love of God)
- **Maka-Tao** (Love of People)
- **Makakalikasan** (Love of Nature)
- **Makabansa** (Love of Country)

### Entering Core Values Grades

1. Click **"Grade Entry"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Core Values"**
3. Select your **section**
4. For each student, rate them on each core value using the marking guide:
   - **AO** — Always Observed
   - **SO** — Sometimes Observed
   - **RO** — Rarely Observed
   - **NO** — Not Observed
5. Click **"Save"**

> **Note:** Core values grades appear on the student's Form 138 (Report Card) alongside academic grades.

## 15. Lesson Plans

**Who can access:** Teacher, Admin, Principal (view)

### Creating a Lesson Plan

1. Click **"Lesson Plans"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Create Lesson Plan"**
3. Fill in the standard DepEd Detailed Lesson Plan (DLP) format:
   - **Subject** and **Grade Level**
   - **Quarter** and **Week**
   - **Learning Competency** (from the curriculum guide)
   - **Objectives** (Knowledge, Skills, Attitude)
   - **Content** / Topic
   - **Learning Resources** / Materials
   - **Procedures:**
     - Reviewing previous lesson
     - Establishing purpose
     - Presenting examples
     - Discussing new concepts
     - Developing mastery
     - Finding practical applications
     - Generalizations
     - Evaluating learning
     - Additional activities for remediation
   - **Remarks** and **Reflection**
4. Click **"Save"**

### AI-Assisted Lesson Plans ✨

EduSync can generate lesson plan suggestions using AI:
1. When creating a lesson plan, click **"Generate with AI"**
2. Enter the **topic** and **learning competency**
3. The system will draft a lesson plan following the DepEd DLP format
4. Review, edit, and save

> **Note:** AI-generated plans are suggestions only. Always review and customize them to fit your specific class needs.

#### Elementary & Junior High School (K-10)

| Form | Official Name | Purpose | When Needed |

| **SF4** | Monthly Movement Report | Transfers, dropouts, returnees | Monthly |

| **SF8** | Health & Nutrition Report | Student health and BMI data | Quarterly |
| **SF9** | Promotion/Retention Report | Per-student promotion status | End of school year |

| Form | Official Name | Purpose |

> **Tip for Teachers:** You can access School Forms too! Use the search bar to quickly jump to the form you need without scrolling through all the cards.

## 18. Form 137 — Permanent Record

**Who can access:** Admin, Registrar, Principal

### What is Form 137?

Form 137 is the **official cumulative academic record** of a student, tracking their grades across all school years. It is required for:
- Student transfers between schools
- Graduation clearance
- College admission

### Generating Form 137

1. Click **"Form 137"** under Reports & Forms in the sidebar
2. The Form 137 Dashboard lists all students
3. Search for a student by name or LRN
4. Click on the student to open their Form 137
5. The system automatically compiles:
   - Student personal data
   - School information (name, address, district, division, region)
   - Academic records per school year (grades per subject per quarter)
   - Certification and signatures

### Printing Form 137

1. Open the student's Form 137
2. Click **"Print"**
3. The form is formatted in the official DepEd layout
4. Use your browser's print dialog to print or save as PDF

5. The form displays in official DepEd format with:

   - Student information
   - Cumulative grades across all eligible school years

### Printing SF10

1. Open the student's SF10
2. Click **"Print"**
3. The system formats it correctly with proper page breaks and DepEd-compliant layout

## 21. ELLN Assessment

**Who can access:** Admin, Teacher, Principal

### What is ELLN?

ELLN (Every Child a Learner Now) is DepEd's program for assessing and improving **reading proficiency** in early grades (K-3).

### ELLN Module Features

| Feature | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Assessment** | Input reading proficiency data for individual learners |
| **Results** | View assessment results and track progress |
| **Reports** | Generate ELLN reports for division submission |
| **ILMP** | Individualized Learning Monitoring Plan for struggling readers |

### Conducting an ELLN Assessment

1. Click **"ELLN Assessment"** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Assessment"** tab
3. Select the student
4. Rate the student on each ELLN competency
5. Click **"Save"**

### Viewing Results

1. Go to **ELLN > Results**
2. Filter by section or grade level
3. The system shows reading proficiency levels:
   - **Frustrated** — Below expected level
   - **Instructional** — Developing
   - **Independent** — At or above expected level

## 29. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### General

**Q: Can I access EduSync on my phone?**  
A: Yes! EduSync works on any device with a web browser. The interface adjusts to fit your screen size.

**Q: Can I use EduSync offline?**  
A: EduSync has limited offline support. Some pages may load from cache, but you need an internet connection to save data and generate reports.

**Q: How is my data protected?**  
A: EduSync uses industry-standard encryption for all data. Each school's data is kept completely separate from other schools. Only authorized users can access the system.

### For Teachers

**Q: I can't see my gradebook. What's wrong?**  
A: Your **teaching assignments** may not be set up yet. Ask your school administrator to assign you to your sections and subjects under Teacher Management. See Section 5 for details.

**Q: Can I edit grades after saving?**  
A: Yes, you can edit grades at any time during the grading period. Contact your admin if grades are locked after the deadline.

**Q: My grade calculations seem wrong. What should I check?**  
A: 
1. Make sure the **Highest Possible Score (HPS)** is correct for each activity in the ECR
2. Check that the **component weights** (WW, PT, QA) are set correctly for the subject
3. Verify that all scores are entered (blank scores may affect the average)

**Q: How do I enter grades for MAPEH?**  
A: MAPEH is a **composite subject** with four components (Music, Arts, PE, Health). Enter grades for each component separately, and the system will auto-compute the MAPEH average.

### For Admins

**Q: How do I add default subjects for a new school?**  
A: Go to **Learning Areas** in the sidebar. If no subjects exist, click **"Load Default DepEd Subjects"** to automatically add all standard subjects based on your school type.

**Q: A teacher account is showing "Missing or insufficient permissions." What do I do?**  
A: Check the teacher's role in **User Management**. Ensure their account has the correct role assigned (e.g., "teacher" not "student").

**Q: How do I change the current school year?**  
A: Go to **Settings** → Update the **Current School Year** field. This affects which academic year is active for grades, attendance, and reports.

### For Students

**Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**  
A: Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page, or ask your teacher/admin to reset your password.

**Q: Why can't I see my grades yet?**  
A: Your teacher may not have entered or published grades yet. Grades become visible once your teacher saves them.

### For Parents

**Q: How do I register for a parent account?**  
A: Go to your school's EduSync website and click **"Parent Registration"**. Fill in your details and link your account to your child using their Student ID (LRN).

**Q: I have multiple children in the school. Do I need separate accounts?**  
A: No! One parent account can be linked to multiple children. You can view all of them from your dashboard.

## 30. Troubleshooting

### Page Won't Load or Is Stuck

1. **Refresh the page** — Press `F5` or `Ctrl + R`
2. **Clear browser cache** — Press `Ctrl + Shift + Delete`, select "Cached images and files", and click "Clear"
3. **Try a different browser** — If Chrome isn't working, try Edge or Firefox
4. **Check your internet** — Make sure you are connected to the internet

### "Missing or Insufficient Permissions" Error

- Your account role may not have access to that page
- Contact your school administrator to verify your role

### Grades Not Showing on Report Cards

1. Make sure grades have been **saved** in the gradebook
2. Check that the correct **school year** and **quarter** are selected
3. Verify that the student is **assigned to a section**
4. Verify that the teacher has **teaching assignments** configured

### Print Layout Issues

1. When printing, use **Chrome or Edge** for best results
2. In the print dialog:
   - Set **Margins** to "None" or "Minimum"
   - Enable **"Background graphics"** checkbox
   - Set **Scale** to "Fit to page" if content is cut off
3. For reports with DepEd headers (logos), make sure "Background graphics" is checked

### Data Not Updating

- EduSync uses real-time data — changes should appear immediately
- If data seems stale, try refreshing the page
- If multiple users are editing the same data, the latest save will take effect

### Can't Find a Feature

- Use the **sidebar menu** to browse all available sections
- Remember: what you see depends on your **role**
  - Teachers don't see Financial Management
  - Students don't see admin tools
- If you think you should have access to a feature you can't find, contact your administrator
