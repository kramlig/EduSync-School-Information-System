**Format:** Vertical (9:16) for Reels/TikTok/Shorts or Horizontal (16:9) for LinkedIn/Website  
**Duration:** 15–20 seconds  
**Tone:** Confident, modern, professional  

---

## SCRIPT

### Scene 1 — Hook (0–3 s)
**Visual:** Close-up of Mark Gil facing camera, clean background (or blurred code editor). Text overlay fades in.  
**Text on screen:** `"I built a school system from scratch."`  
**Audio/VO:**  
> "I built a school system from scratch."

---

### Scene 2 — Introduction (3–8 s)
**Visual:** Medium shot of Mark Gil. Subtle animated lower-third appears.  
**Lower-third text:**  
`Mark Gil Dotillos`  
`Founder & Software Engineer — EduSync`  
**Audio/VO:**  
> "I'm Mark Gil Dotillos — founder and software engineer behind EduSync."

---

### Scene 3 — Product Showcase (8–16 s)
**Visual:** Quick screen recordings (2–3 fast cuts):  
1. Dashboard with live student/teacher metrics  
2. Gradebook or SF9 Report Card view  
3. Student list with search/filter in action  

**Text overlay (optional):** `K-12 School Information System · DepEd-Compliant · Cloud-Based`  
**Audio/VO:**  
> "EduSync is a cloud-based school information system — managing students, grades, attendance, and official DepEd forms for Philippine K-12 schools."

---

### Scene 4 — CTA / Close (16–20 s)
**Visual:** Logo + URL on screen. Mark Gil nods or gives a quick confident look.  
**Text on screen:**  
`edusync.ph`  
`The future of school management.`  
**Audio/VO:**  
> "This is the future of school management."

---

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Background music:** Upbeat, modern lo-fi or tech-style instrumental (royalty-free)  
- **Pacing:** Keep cuts tight — no dead air. Match cuts to beat drops if using music.  
- **Screen recordings:** Use the demo at `https://edusync.ph` with Super Admin credentials  
- **Wardrobe:** Smart casual or plain dark shirt — keep it clean and founder-like  
- **Lighting:** Natural light or ring light, avoid harsh shadows
