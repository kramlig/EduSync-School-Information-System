EduSync Logo Generator

Generate a placeholder logo for your EduSync system

📋 Instructions:

  1. Click "Generate New Logo" to create a logo
  2. Click "Download as PNG" to save the image
  3. Rename the downloaded file to edusync-logo.png
  4. Copy it to your project's public/ folder
  5. Refresh your application to see the logo!

OR - If you have your own logo image:

  1. Save your logo as edusync-logo.png
  2. Copy it to the public/ folder
  3. Make sure it's a PNG with transparent background for best results
